# Privacy Policy — Techno Staff

**Last updated: 2026-04-28**

---

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Techno Team ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Techno Staff mobile application ("the App"). By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

---

## Data we collect

**Information you provide:**

- **Name and email address** — when your account is created by an administrator.
- **Role** — whether you are an admin or employee.
- **Task data** — titles, descriptions, due dates, priorities, status updates, and assignment information.

**Information collected automatically:**

- **FCM (Firebase Cloud Messaging) token** — generated by your device to enable push notifications. Updated each time you sign in.
- **Timestamps** — account creation time, task creation/update/completion times.
- **Account status** (`isActive`) — tracked to control access.

---

## How we use the data

- To display tasks, assignments, and progress information to you and your team.
- To send push notifications about new task assignments, status changes, deadlines, and overdue tasks.
- To maintain an audit trail of task activity (who created or updated a task and when).
- Firebase automatically collects basic analytics and crash data as part of the infrastructure; we do not use this data for advertising.

---

## Where data is stored

All data is stored in Google Firebase services (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging) within the `techno-staff` Firebase project, hosted on Google Cloud infrastructure. Data is stored in the region assigned by Firebase at project creation.

---

## Retention

Your data is retained until you delete your account. When you delete your account:

- Your `users` profile document and all personal notifications are permanently deleted.
- Tasks you created or were assigned to remain accessible to the team (the tasks themselves are business records), but your display name on those tasks is replaced with "Deleted user". Your account UID may persist in those task records for audit purposes.

---

## Sharing with third parties

We do not sell or share your personal data with marketing partners or advertisers. Your data is shared only with Google / Firebase as our infrastructure provider, subject to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

---

## Your rights

- **Access** — you can view all task and account data within the App.
- **Correction** — contact your team administrator to correct your name or email.
- **Deletion** — you can delete your account at any time by opening **Settings → Account → Delete account**. Deletion is immediate and irreversible. If you cannot access the App, contact us at `moh95od@gmail.com` to request manual deletion.

---

## Children's data

Techno Staff is intended for workplace use by adults. We do not knowingly collect data from users under 16 years of age. If you believe a minor's data has been collected, please contact us at `moh95od@gmail.com` and we will remove it promptly.

---

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Significant changes will be communicated the next time you open the App. Continued use after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
